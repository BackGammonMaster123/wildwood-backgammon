PR (performance rating) is the standard skill measure from eXtreme Gammon: average equity lost per non-forced decision ×500. Bands: under 2.5 world champion · 5 world class · 7.5 expert · 12.5 advanced · 17.5 intermediate · 22.5 casual · 30 beginner. Here the coach does the judging (it is trained to copy the wildbg engine), so treat it as a yardstick against your own past games. Rating uses the FIBS formula against the bot levels (Easy 500, Medium 1700, Strong 2200): a money game counts as a 1-point match, a match counts once when it ends. You start at the rating of the first level you play.
